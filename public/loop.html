Lesson 1: How to talk to AI so it builds what you actually want

Ever asked an AI to "build me an app" and gotten something generic? That's not the AI being lazy. That's it guessing.

A vague prompt gives the AI a thousand directions and no destination. So it picks the average of all of them — a login page, a dashboard, dummy data. An app that could be anyone's.

FOR [your person]
THAT [the job it does for them]
WITH [3–5 specific features]
Weak: "build me a booking app"

Strong: "Build a booking app FOR busy salon owners THAT lets clients book, reschedule, and pay a deposit WITH automatic reminder texts, a calendar owners can see at a glance, and a cancelation list that fills open slots automatically."

Same AI. Different result. The formula isn't magic — it's you deciding, in advance, what "right" looks like. The AI stops guessing and starts building your app.

One trap to avoid: tell the AI what to BUILD, not what you're looking at. Pasting a page of text from another app makes it build what's on the page — not what's in your head.
